# Weirdest job title candidates (draft pass)

One job title each, at most 60 characters; blind-judged
pairwise on weirder and funnier.

1. Spreadsheet vibes: "Chief Vibes Officer for a
   Spreadsheet" -- corporate nonsense at executive rank.
2. Almost-finished: "Assistant Deputy Manager of
   Almost-Finished Projects" -- a full hierarchy of
   incompletion; the weirder because it is the most
   plausible.
3. Silence: "Vice President of Holding the Elevator in
   Silence" -- a whole job of one awkward duty.
4. Beige: "Senior Beige Consultant (Neutrality Division)"
   -- colour as a department.
5. Left-handed: "Director of Left-Handed Scissors
   Procurement, EMEA" -- specificity as comedy; the region
   makes it.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2. Blind pairwise judging rewards the
title that gets weirder the longer it is read, and
candidate 2 is three nested diminutions -- Assistant, to a
Deputy, of a Manager -- ruling over a portfolio defined by
its incompletion: Almost-Finished Projects. It is also,
devastatingly, the most plausible title in the set, which
is what makes it the funniest.
